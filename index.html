Play fair, finish proud

The Canuckle hint ladder

The best hint is the smallest one that gets you moving again. Climb one rung at a time and stop as soon as the word clicks.

1

Re-read your tiles

Write down every red and gold letter and where it cannot go. Half of all “stuck” moments end here.

2

Think about the topic

Is it an animal, a food, a place or a bit of slang? The Canadian theme cuts the list down fast.

3

Count the vowels

Knowing whether the word has one, two or three vowels rules out whole families of words.

4

Check for repeats

GOOSE, TAFFY or POPPY hide a double letter that is easy to miss.

5

Use the first letter

The strongest clue of all – save it for your last one or two guesses.

6

Look up the answer

No shame in it. Read the fun fact and you will know the word next time.

Inside the game

The three built-in Canuckle hints

01

FAQ

Canuckle hint & answer questions

Where can I find today’s Canuckle answer?

The official daily hints and today’s answer are published on the Canuckle hints page of canukle.com. This helper gives you gentle clues first, so you can still solve the puzzle yourself.

Does this helper know today’s answer?

No. It works only from the guesses and colours you enter and compares them with a list of Canadian-themed five-letter words. If the answer is not on that list, the helper will tell you.

How do I enter my tile colours?

Type your guess, add it, then tap each tile until it matches the colour you saw in the game: grey for not in the word, gold for the wrong spot and red for the right spot.

Will using hints spoil the puzzle?

Only if you want it to. The hints are revealed one step at a time, a spoiler meter shows how much you have uncovered, and the possible words stay hidden until you press reveal.

What is the best first word for Canuckle?

Words with several common vowels and consonants, such as AROSE or RAISE, rule out the most answers. CANOE is a popular Canadian-themed opener that tests four vowels at once.

What are the official Canuckle hints?

Inside the game, the hint button reveals three clues one by one: how many vowels the word has, whether a letter repeats, and the first letter.

Why does the helper show no words?

Usually one tile colour was entered incorrectly. Check every tile against the game. If they are correct, today’s answer is simply not in the helper’s word list.

Is it cheating to use hints?

Canuckle is a game for fun, so how much help you use is up to you. Many players use a single small hint to keep their streak going and still enjoy solving the rest themselves.
